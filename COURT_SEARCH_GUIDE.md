# Arabic search for court cases: step-by-step guide

This guide is for the manual Arabic search that was still needed after the first list of cases (`data/court_cases.csv`). The online search found no Saudi or Kuwaiti judgments on the sea, few Sharia rulings, and only news reports for most other national cases. Most court websites in the region block automated reading or are only in Arabic, so a person needs to search them by hand.

Work through the tasks in order. Task 1 matters most. Spend about 30 to 45 minutes on each task. If nothing turns up in that time, write "searched, nothing found" and move on. That result is useful too.

## What to write down for each case you find

Add one line per case to `data/court_cases.csv`, or send the details in any form and we will add them. We need:

1. Court and city.
2. Case number and year, in Hijri and Gregorian if both are given.
3. Date of the judgment.
4. The parties (or "names withheld").
5. What happened, in one or two sentences.
6. What the court decided.
7. **How the court reasons and settles the case**, in a few lines of your own words, with short Arabic quotes where they help. Religious words are not needed: most judgments will not use them, and the spirit of the Islamic legal tradition often shows in other ways. Signs worth noting:
   - weighing harm against the public good, or preventing harm before it happens;
   - mercy or leniency, for example a plea for forgiveness, a first offence, or a suspended sentence;
   - settling the dispute to restore relations (صلح، تسوية), not only to punish;
   - attention to the person: their standing, intent, family or livelihood;
   - repair of the damage, such as clean-up costs or restoring the site, alongside or instead of punishment;
   - deference to custom, local practice or the ruler's decree as the source of the rule;
   - the judge's own judgment of what is fair where the law is silent.
8. Any mention of an international treaty (for example the UN law of the sea treaty, اتفاقية الأمم المتحدة لقانون البحار, or the Kuwait Convention, اتفاقية الكويت الإقليمية).
9. A link, or a photo or PDF of the page.

## Search words to use everywhere

Try these one at a time in each portal. Short words work better than long phrases.

| Topic | Arabic search words |
| --- | --- |
| Pollution | تلويث البحر، التلوث البحري، تسرب نفطي، بقعة زيت، تصريف الزيت، مياه الصابورة، البيئة البحرية |
| Fishing | صيد، الثروات المائية الحية، الثروة البحرية، شباك محظورة، الصيد الجائر، موسم الحظر، الربيان / الروبيان، القراقير، الحضرة، الغزل |
| Protected nature | محمية، محمية طبيعية، السلاحف، الشعاب المرجانية، القرم، الأحياء الفطرية |
| Coast and land filling | ردم، دفان البحر، الشاطئ، حرم الشاطئ، الواجهة البحرية، الأملاك العامة، الشاليهات |
| Sea limits | خفر السواحل، المياه الإقليمية، المنطقة الاقتصادية الخالصة |
| Pearling and custom | الغوص، اللؤلؤ، المغاصات، الهيرات، السالفة، النوخذة، الطواش |

These words are only for finding cases. Once you have a case, read the whole reasoning, not just the words.
| Law numbers | the number of each country's environment or fisheries law, for example "24 لسنة 1999" (UAE environment), "42 لسنة 2014" (Kuwait environment) |

## Task 1. Saudi Arabia (no cases found yet)

All Saudi general courts apply Sharia, so any Saudi judgment on the sea also answers the Sharia question.

1. **Ministry of Justice, Collection of Judicial Rulings (مجموعة الأحكام القضائية), 1434 AH and 1435 AH.** The 1434 set has 30 volumes. The index in volume 30 covers only volumes 1 to 9, which are civil cases, and has nothing on the sea: https://moj.gov.sa/ar-sa/ministry/versions/Documents/backupFiles3/AhkamGroup_1434/30.pdf . Open the indexes of the other volumes, especially the criminal and ta'zir volumes (القضايا الجزائية، التعزيرية), and look for fishing, coast guard and pollution cases. Then do the same for the 1435 set.
2. **Board of Grievances (ديوان المظالم), Collection of Administrative Rulings and Principles (مجموعة الأحكام والمبادئ الإدارية).** These yearly volumes hold cases against government bodies. Look for challenges to fines from the environment ministry (وزارة البيئة والمياه والزراعة), refused fishing licences, and coastal land or sea-filling disputes (ردم، أراضٍ ساحلية) in Jeddah, Qatif, Jazan and Farasan.
3. **The Ministry of Justice Scientific Judicial Portal (البوابة القضائية العلمية)** on moj.gov.sa. It may need a free account. Search with the words in the table above.
4. **Al-Qadaiya journal (مجلة القضائية)**, also on the Ministry of Justice site (adlm.moj.gov.sa). It prints selected judgments with comments.

## Task 2. Kuwait (no cases found yet)

1. **Constitutional Court rulings**, published in volumes on cck.moj.gov.kw (for example "المجلد السادس - الجزء الثاني"). Look for any challenge to the Environment Protection Law 42/2014 or its 2015 amendment (قانون 99 لسنة 2015). Search "عدم دستورية" with "البيئة".
2. **Kuwait Institute for Judicial and Legal Studies (kijls.moj.gov.kw)**. Its monthly bulletins of new Court of Cassation rulings ("المستحدث", one PDF per month, for example "المستحدث 2023") can each be searched with Ctrl+F for البيئة، البحر، الصيد، الشاليهات.
3. **Two specific events to look for:**
   - The mass fish deaths in Kuwait Bay in 2001 (نفوق الأسماك في جون الكويت 2001). Did fishermen or their union (اتحاد الصيادين) sue the government?
   - Coastal chalets on state land (الشاليهات، أملاك الدولة، الخيران، بنيدر). These are cases about who owns the shore.
4. **Law journals with case comments (تعليق على حكم):** the Kuwait International Law School Journal (journal.kilaw.edu.kw) and the Kuwait University Journal of Law.

## Task 3. United Arab Emirates

1. **Federal Supreme Court judgments on the Ministry of Justice site (moj.gov.ae).** The judgments are PDFs named like "الطعن رقم 173 لسنة 2025 إداري". The site blocks automated reading but opens in a normal browser. Search for "24 لسنة 1999" (environment law) and "23 لسنة 1999" (fisheries law).
2. **Ras Al Khaimah Public Prosecution (rakpp.rak.ae).** It publishes full judgments. We found one there already (appeal 16 of year 2, 2007, "تلويث البحر بالزيت"). Search the same site for صيد and محمية.
3. **Abu Dhabi Judicial Department and Dubai Courts.** Both publish legal principles (المبادئ القانونية) from their Courts of Cassation. Search with the words in the table above.
4. **Follow up two cases already on the list:**
   - The verdict in the 2019 collision and oil spill off Fujairah and Kalba (search Emarat Al Youm or Al Khaleej: "الفجيرة تصادم سفينتين بقعة زيت حكم").
   - The result of the Ministry of Environment's AED 20 million civil claim over the tanker White Whale in Umm Al Quwain (search "الحوت الأبيض أم القيوين").

## Task 4. Bahrain

1. **Constitutional Court rulings.** These are printed in the Official Gazette, which is online at the Legislation and Legal Opinion Commission (lloc.gov.bh). Search "المحكمة الدستورية" with البيئة or الثروة البحرية (the marine wealth law is Decree-Law 20/2002).
2. **Land filling and fishermen.** Look for court claims by fishermen for loss of fishing grounds through sea filling (تعويض الصيادين، الدفان، فشت العظم). The archive of Al Wasat newspaper is a good place to start.
3. **The pearling court (محكمة السالفة).** Read the Arabic pages of British Library file IOR/R/15/2/1189 on the Qatar Digital Library: https://www.qdl.qa/en/archive/81055/vdc_100000000241.0x00035c . Note any named disputes, who sat as judges, and how decisions were reached.

## Task 5. Qatar

1. **Al Meezan (almeezan.qa).** The Ministry of Justice relaunched it on 11 October 2026 with a new search. Look in its judgments section with the words in the table above.
2. **Find Arabic reports of the 2018 case of the Bahraini fishermen** fined in Qatar (Al Raya, Al Sharq, or Bahrain's Al Watan: "صيادين بحرينيين قطر غرامة 2018"). We want the court's name, the case number and the charge.

## Task 6. Oman

1. **Supreme Judicial Council (sjc.gov.om), "Legal principles" collections.** These are PDFs of principles from the Supreme Court, including one for the criminal division ("مجموعة المبادئ الصادرة من الدائرة الجزائية"). Search each with Ctrl+F for صيد، الثروة المائية، التلوث، محمية.
2. **The Administrative Court (محكمة القضاء الإداري)** also publishes principles. Look for cases on fishing licences and coastal land grants.
3. **The 2026 turtle case at Ras Al Hadd (Sur Court of First Instance).** Find the Arabic report (Oman Daily or Al Watan) and note which law the tourists were charged under. It is probably the nature reserves and wildlife law (قانون المحميات الطبيعية وصون الأحياء الفطرية), but this is not yet checked.

## Task 7. Older Sharia and customary rulings on the sea

1. **The Qatar Digital Library (qdl.qa)** holds Arabic letters and rulings by qadis and rulers on pearling debts, diving crews and fishing traps. Search the Arabic interface for السالفة، الغوص، الطواويش، حكم القاضي.
2. **Kuwait and the UAE** hold similar records in their national archives: the Kuwait Centre for Research and Studies (مركز البحوث والدراسات الكويتية) and the UAE National Library and Archives (agda.ae). Only note what is easy to find. A full archive search is a separate project.

## If you have a university login

The paid databases eastlaws.com (شبكة قوانين الشرق), Mandumah (دار المنظومة) and Al Manhal hold many Gulf judgments and case comments that are not free online. One search on each with the words in the table above may find more than all the free portals together.
